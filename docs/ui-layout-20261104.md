# Workspace layout update — 4 November 2026

The update reorganizes Home, adds a wide-screen workspace sidebar, and gives the
analysis page primary and secondary columns. It preserves the established teal
design, routes, account data, analysis calculations and AI consent controls.

## Implemented

- Home: one date control and upload entry; selected-day diary and recording first;
  seven-day diary and recording plans next; history and knowledge links retained.
- Navigation: sidebar at 72rem and above; existing section navigation on tablets
  and narrower desktops; existing bottom navigation below 48rem. Wellbeing links
  delegate to its existing save/discard guard and retain recording/date context.
- Analysis: waveforms and windows first; AI entry, recording information and notes
  alongside when the analysis container is at least 60rem, otherwise below.
  Section anchors and narrow-container waveform controls improve navigation.

## Verification

- 119 frontend/component tests across 13 files passed, including three new
  sidebar tests. TypeScript checking and the production build passed.
- Home and analysis were inspected in English and Chinese at 320, 375, 414, 768,
  1024 and 1440 CSS-pixel widths. No document-wide horizontal overflow was found.
- With the desktop sidebar and AI panel open, the analysis container measured
  753px and correctly used one column; the 619px window table did not overflow
  its scroll container. Home also stacked its cards when space narrowed.
- Screenshots and measurements are in `.tmp/ui-layout-20261104/`, excluded from
  Git. The local browser uses a synthetic cached fixture and rejects all writes,
  uploads, worker requests and AI requests. These checks do not establish live
  account, model, email or real-device acceptance.
